## About Me

I'm a final-year **Electronics and Communication Engineering** student at Vidya Jyothi Institute of Technology, Hyderabad — currently maintaining a CGPA of **8.95**.

My background sits at the intersection of electronics and software. Over time, I've grown increasingly drawn to the software side — specifically, building things that people can actually use. I enjoy the full loop: identifying a problem, thinking through the system, and shipping something that works.

What defines how I work:

- **Rapid prototyping** — I move fast, build proof-of-concepts, and iterate. Speed of learning matters more than perfection on the first pass.
- **Systems thinking** — Before writing a single line of code, I try to understand the whole picture: inputs, outputs, failure modes, and interactions between parts.
- **Hands-on experimentation** — Whether it's a web app, a microcontroller, or a protocol, I learn best by building and breaking things in practice.
- **Cross-domain comfort** — Years spent on both hardware and software mean I naturally think about how the two sides connect, and I'm comfortable jumping between them.

---

## What I Do Day-to-Day

On most days, you'll find me doing one or more of these:

- **Building and shipping** small applications or tools — functional, usable, and focused on solving a real problem
- **Experimenting with new ideas** — spinning up a quick prototype to test whether something works before committing to it fully
- **Reading and researching** — staying current with what's happening in web development, embedded systems, and the broader tech space
- **Debugging** — whether it's a JavaScript logic error or a microcontroller behaving unexpectedly, tracking down root causes is something I genuinely enjoy
- **Thinking through system design** — sketching out how components of a system interact, even for simple personal projects
- **Writing clean, readable code** — I care about code that other people (and future me) can understand without a guide
